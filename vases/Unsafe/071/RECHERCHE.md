Gallé Soufflé Vase.

Circa 1930.

The soufflé vase is decorated with a frieze of purple cameo clematis flower heads created with the soufflé, or blow-out, technique, set on a light pink base ground.

Marks:
Cameo signature Gallé to side.

Dimensions:
16.5 centimetres high.

Notes:
Soufflé pieces were made at the Gallé works from the mid 1920s to the mid 1930s.
