Design

## Beauty robed in cool velvet

The three crystal vases from the creator’s collection ‘Cool Velvet’ draw their pleasantly cool touches from the deep tones of aquamarine and beryl, underlaid with amethyst. Although each vase has found inspiration in a certain geometric structure, they have far to go before succumbing to mathematic bluntness. Their design is playful, refined and full of unanticipated meaning. They refresh with their multifaceted appearance, and caress with the cultivated beauty of cut glass.

Designer

## Katerina Dousova

Katerina is a Czech designer specialising in product and interior design. She studied architecture and interior design at the Academy of Arts, Architecture & Design (UMPRUM) in Prague. Besides product design, she focuses on the renovations of historic interiors, for example the well-known Café Louvre in Prague. She has been collaborating with Moser since 1999. She managed to follow up on the successful Moser production from 1970, using traditional Moser glassmaking techniques.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
