ZAHA HADID ARTWORK

For engravers, capturing architectural forms in glass – whether historic or contemporary – is always a significant challenge. Moser engraver Radek Svoboda has masterfully rendered in crystal the work of world-renowned architect Zaha Hadid.

ANTONÍN DVOŘÁK PRIZE

Since 2009, Moser glassmakers have made the prize for the international music event Dvořák Prague Festival. Laureates win this award for outstanding artistic accomplishments and for promoting Czech classical music. The piece was designed by architect and designer Jiří Pelcl, whose works feature in significant museums worldwide, including MoMA in New York. The prize takes the form of a violoncello – a clear reference to one of Dvořák’s most famous compositions, Cello Concerto in B minor, op. 104 – presented in the cubist style.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
