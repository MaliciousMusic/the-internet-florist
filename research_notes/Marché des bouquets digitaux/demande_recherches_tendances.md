# Search demand & social-media interest: digital bouquets, virtual flowers, animated flower e-cards (as of 2 Oct 2026)

## Q1. Estimated monthly search volumes (global, US, France), derived daily averages and seasonal peaks

### Takeaway
In absolute terms the demand is small. In third-party tools, no keyword in this group gets more than about 1,000–3,000 searches a month: "virtual flowers" has about 1,200/month in the US (SpyFu) and "digital flowers" about 2,900/month worldwide (RankHero). The French keywords are close to nothing: "bouquet virtuel" gets about 50/month in France (Ubersuggest), and the other French terms are below Google Trends' reporting threshold. Google Trends also shows that interest in Jan–Sep 2026 is about 7× ("virtual flowers") to about 90× ("digital bouquet") the 2023 level. The tool estimates often lag, so they probably understate the 2026 surge. The peaks are Valentine's week (Feb 2026) and Mar–May 2026 (around Mother's Day). Estimates for the same keyword vary by up to about 36× between tools.

### Inferences
- **Anchored estimates from Google Trends, worldwide (my calculation; very uncertain).** Method: take the weekly worldwide Google Trends index for the 5 terms on one chart, convert it to monthly means, and scale it with RankHero's monthly "digital flowers" volumes for Sep 2025–Aug 2026 (total 38,300). The resulting factor is about 104 searches per index-point-month, but the per-month factor ranges from 66 to 189. On that basis, the 12-month averages (Sep 2025–Aug 2026) are:
  - "digital bouquet" ≈ 930/month (range ≈590–1,700), ≈31/day.
  - "virtual flowers" ≈ 780/month (500–1,400), ≈26/day.
  - "digibouquet" ≈ 740/month (470–1,350), ≈25/day. Nearly all of it falls in Feb–Aug 2026.
  - "virtual bouquet" ≈ 190/month (120–340), ≈6/day.
  - "digital flowers" ≈ 3,190/month, ≈106/day (the anchor itself).
- **Peak-month estimates.** Feb 2026: "digibouquet" ≈ 3,200 (≈115/day), "digital bouquet" ≈ 1,300, "virtual flowers" ≈ 1,100. Apr 2026: "digital bouquet" ≈ 2,000 (≈66/day). On 14 Feb 2026 alone, "digibouquet" probably got about 1,200 searches worldwide. This uses the daily Google Trends index (digibouquet = 3.6× "digital flowers" that day) scaled with the Feb "digital flowers" volume.
- These estimates are likely conservative for brand-type terms such as "digibouquet". Google Trends' broad match inflates the anchor ("digital flowers" matches any query containing both words), which pushes the scaled estimates for other terms down.
- **The tool estimates (SpyFu, Google-provided via SpyFu, Ubersuggest) probably lag the 2026 surge.** Google Trends shows Jan–Sep 2026 interest at about 7–90× 2023 levels, depending on the term (Q2). The Google-provided figures on SpyFu (e.g., "digital bouquet" 20/month in the US) look like pre-trend snapshots. A realistic 2026 range for "digital bouquet" worldwide is the high hundreds to about 2,000 searches a month at peaks. This is a judgment, not a measured figure.
- **French order of magnitude.** On the France Google Trends chart, "bouquet virtuel" (≈50/month per Ubersuggest) averages below 0.5. That implies 1 index point > ~100 searches/month, which gives rough lower bounds: "e-carte" (avg 5) > ~500/month, "carte animée" (avg 9) > ~900/month, "carte virtuelle" (avg 29) > ~2,900/month. "carte virtuelle" is ambiguous: it also covers virtual bank cards, and its New-Year peaks point to greeting cards. These are order-of-magnitude bounds, not estimates. All French "digital bouquet"-type terms are probably in the tens of searches a month outside Valentine's week.

### Gaps
- No free source gave French-market volumes for "bouquet digital", "fleurs virtuelles", "carte virtuelle fleurs", "e-carte" or "carte virtuelle". Only one anonymous Ubersuggest lookup was possible. Semrush, KWFinder and Ubersuggest beyond one lookup need accounts, and Ahrefs' free generator needs a CAPTCHA. Google Keyword Planner was not accessible.
- No tool gave a volume for "digibouquet" (SpyFu and RankHero: no data), even though it is the term behind the Feb 2026 Google Trends peak.
- RankHero's data source and data date for "digital bouquet" and "digital flowers" are not stated on the page, and it is unclear whether its "global" figure is really global. The Keyword-Planner-bucket match is my inference.
- Separate US vs global splits were not available for most keywords. SpyFu is US-only (plus Google-provided global figures, which look stale).

## Q2. Google Trends patterns 2023–2026: spikes (DigiBouquet, Valentine's, Mother's Day / Fête des mères) and rising related queries

### Takeaway
Before mid-2025, interest in digital or virtual flowers worldwide was almost flat, with only small Valentine's and Mother's Day bumps. It stepped up in August 2025, when the free DigiBouquet web tool launched and spread. It then surged in 2026: the absolute peak was Valentine's week (8–14 Feb 2026, led by searches for "digibouquet" by name), followed by a long Mar–May plateau around Mother's Day and a late-summer bump. The interest comes mostly from the Philippines, South and Southeast Asia, the US and the UK. France registers only Valentine's 2026. The classic e-card terms are flat or declining in both the US and France.

### Inferences
- The demand seems to be driven by social sharing of free indie web tools: DigiBouquet (Aug 2025), then theBouquet, flowerisblooming and others. Organic search follows the social spikes. People search the brand name ("digibouquet", "digibouquet com/vercel") more than generic phrases. This fits a viral, link-driven product, not search-led demand.
- Three seasonal moments matter: Valentine's (sharpest spike, peaking on the day itself and decaying within a week), Mother's Day in May (the longest and highest plateau for generic "digital flowers") and a late-summer bump in 2026. The Apr–May 2026 plateau may be partly Asian school-year events or the run-up to Mother's Day. That is a hypothesis; the data does not identify the cause.
- French demand for "digital bouquet"-type searches is negligible apart from Saint-Valentin 2026. The French "bouquet virtuel" blips in late April/early May in 2023, 2024 and 2025 might reflect 1er mai (muguet) greetings. This is unverified and the spikes may just be noise.
- Classic e-card search interest is declining (US "ecards" about −34% from 2023 to 2026 YTD; FR "e-carte" falling), while "digital flowers" and "digital bouquet" are growing from a tiny base. In US Google Trends, "digital flowers" overtook "ecards" in April 2026. This is broad match, so it signals the direction rather than proving it.
- The main growth geographies are the Philippines, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, Malaysia and Indonesia for "digibouquet", and the US, UK, Germany and the Netherlands for generic "digital flowers".

### Gaps
- Google Trends gives only relative, broad-match values, and the weekly France data is mostly below the reporting threshold. There is no reliable Fête des mères signal for French terms.
- The cause of the Apr 2026 "digital bouquet" peak and the Sep 2026 "digital flowers" bump was not identified.
- I found no mainstream news coverage that quantifies the DigiBouquet trend. The evidence is the creator's own posts, Google Trends and TikTok data.
- Google Trends' new Gemini-based Explore was not used. Daily data was pulled only for Feb 2026.

## Q4. CPC and keyword difficulty (commercial value and competition)

### Takeaway
Commercial value is very low. Digital or virtual flower keywords have no or almost no advertisers (0–2 paid competitors in the US) and CPCs from $0 to about $0.79 exact match, with a single broad-match outlier of $3.93. SEO difficulty is easy (SpyFu 5–24/100; Ubersuggest SD 18 for "bouquet virtuel"). Real-flower keywords ("send flowers": $3.42–4.70 CPC, 93 advertisers, difficulty 83) and classic e-cards ("ecards": $1.29–1.36 CPC, 47 advertisers, difficulty 63) are far more contested. The search results are already crowded with free indie tools (DigiBouquet and its clones, theBouquet, Attached).

### Inferences
- **Paid search is weak for monetization here.** The keywords are cheap and mostly free of advertisers, which means nobody earns much from paid search on them. Searchers expect a free tool ("free virtual flowers" is the top related query).
- **Organic search is easy but small.** Ranking organically should be easy (difficulty ≤25), but even #1 positions earn only tens to a few hundred US clicks a month.
- **Adjacent paid intent exists.** Florists bidding on "virtual flowers", and much higher CPCs on e-card and real-flower keywords, suggest the commercial value sits next door: upselling to real-flower delivery, premium e-cards, or Valentine's/Mother's Day gift add-ons.
- **The RankHero $2.01 CPC for "digital flowers" conflicts with SpyFu** (no CPC, 0 advertisers). It may be a Keyword-Planner top-of-page bid estimate from a different period. Treat it as uncertain.

### Gaps
- No Semrush or Ahrefs keyword difficulty (login or CAPTCHA). The SpyFu, Ubersuggest and RankHero difficulty scales are not comparable, and RankHero's is Etsy-specific.
- No CPC or difficulty data for French terms other than "bouquet virtuel" (€0.00, SD 18). France was not available in SpyFu.
- No Google Keyword Planner bid ranges were accessed.
