# Existing "digital bouquet" / virtual-flower offerings: inventory as of 2 October 2026

*Method note: all prices, ratings, download bands and traffic figures were seen on **2026-10-02** unless another date is stated. Data came from web search and page fetches, the Apple iTunes Search/Lookup API (US and FR storefronts), App Store and Google Play pages, Etsy, Fiverr and Gumroad search pages read in a browser (Etsy France locale in EUR plus a USD locale override), Similarweb public pages (August 2026 data), and the GitHub search API. ## Q1. Which viral free digital-bouquet generator websites exist (DigiBouquet, its clones and forks, other "build a bouquet" sites that went viral on TikTok in 2025-2026)? Who made them, how much traffic did they get, and do they make money?

### Takeaway
DigiBouquet (digibouquet.vercel.app, by illustrator Pauline Wee) started the format around August 2025. It peaked at Valentine's 2026, when it went down after reaching Vercel's 1M edge-request mark, and it still drew about 594K visits in August 2026. It has no visible monetization. Dozens of named copycats and a few hundred hobby forks followed. Only a handful have real traffic: digibouquet.net (~268K), theBouquet (~138K, Philippines-led), Flority (~85K) and FlowerIsBlooming/"Flowers" (~76K). Only theBouquet and FlowerIsBlooming/"Flowers" clearly charge money, with micro-prices: $1.99–$8.99 one-off passes, or a $8.99/week to $39.99/year app subscription.

### Inferences
- DigiBouquet's virality was organic and creator-led: TikTok "how to" videos and the "cute internet spot" genre. The traffic mix (India, US, Philippines, Indonesia) points to Gen-Z and long-distance-relationship use in English-speaking Asia. **France appears in none of the top-country lists retrieved** (the top 3 to top 5 for each generator checked).
- Monetization is nearly absent. The original runs at a cost (the creator paid to upgrade Vercel hosting). The only proven paid tiers are tiny one-off passes (theBouquet, $1.99–$8.99) and an app subscription (Flowers). The 2025-2026 clones copy the name "DigiBouquet" for SEO (digibouquet.net, .org, .space, digi-bouquet.com, and same-named apps). Most rely on organic search (digibouquet.net 70%, theBouquet 60%, Attached 65%), so traffic is captured by name-squatting rather than brand.
- The 1M edge-request mark corresponds to Vercel's Hobby-tier monthly allowance (my reading, not stated in the post). That suggests about 1M+ requests per month around early February 2026.
- Features are converging on the same set: link plus QR sharing, an animated reveal (envelope, gift box or falling petals), and premium music, voice notes and photos. Procedural or AI generation is the new frontier: DigiBouquet AI, BloomNote and AI Bouquet (see Q2/Q4).

### Gaps
- No public figure exists for the number of bouquets created on DigiBouquet. The 1M edge-request mark is a request count, not users. No press interview with Pauline Wee was found.
- Similarweb page summaries were ambiguous about whether figures are one month or the last three months. Treat them as orders of magnitude. Similarweb did not return data for onlinebouquet.com or thedigitalbouquet.com, which are probably too small.
- TikTok hashtag post and view totals for #digibouquet could not be read (the tag page needs login). The discover-page counters are unlabelled.
- The relationship between the `sauravsid/digibouquet` repo and Pauline's site, and Name.com's role (sponsor or registrar?), are unverified.

## Q2. Which mobile apps on the App Store and Google Play offer digital bouquets or virtual flowers to send? Names, ratings, downloads, pricing and in-app purchases.

### Takeaway
Dedicated "send a digital bouquet" apps are numerous but tiny: roughly 15–20 live listings, most launched in 2026 with 0–73 ratings. The only one with modest scale is "Flowers Digital Gift & Invites" (4.81★ from 73 US ratings; 10K+ Android). Older names such as Flower Garden (2009, "20 million bouquets sent") are dormant. Apps monetize mainly through subscriptions ($2.99–$15.99 a month, $19.99–$39.99 a year), one-off unlocks ($4.99) or per-flower IAP ($0.99–$9.99). The 2024 "flower language" craze (name spelled with flowers) produced the largest downloads: up to 1M+.

This is a pivot away from virtual cards. — [kisseo.fr](https://www.kisseo.fr/); [roses-et-orchidees.com](https://www.roses-et-orchidees.com/services-en-ligne-pour-envoyer-des-fleurs-virtuelles-par-e-mail/)

### Inferences
- No venture-scale digital-bouquet startup was found. Every paid offer is solo or micro-team, with prices anchored near e-card levels ($2–$10 per gift) or toy-app subscriptions. Free viral generators cap willingness to pay.
- The NFT wave (2022–2023) tested premium pricing ($25–$275+) but was tied to Valentine's drops and NFT-market sentiment. No recurring NFT bouquet business was identified in 2026.
- Recurring failure patterns: free substitutes everywhere; seasonality (Valentine's and Mother's Day); hosting costs at viral spikes (DigiBouquet's Vercel limit); dependence on platform policy (Facebook); and SEO clones diluting brand.
- The open premium gaps are **generative or unique composition with ownership or authenticity** (NFT-like but without crypto friction), **music or voice**, and **permanence**. theBouquet already sells "everlasting" bouquets as a premium perk.

### Gaps
- Bloomsie's prices, founders and status could not be verified (the domain did not resolve).
- META FLORIST's current catalogue and sales volumes, Flowerboy's sell-through and BloomThis's NFT price were not found.
- No public revenue figures exist for any digital-bouquet product. Closure reasons for They're Beautiful and Flowers2mail are unknown.
- AR flower lenses on Snapchat and Instagram were not inventoried; they are usually free creator lenses.

## Q6. Estimated number of offerings per category and overall (objective synthesis)

### Takeaway
I estimate **about 120 distinct, publicly discoverable digital-bouquet or virtual-flower products and offers** worldwide (range ~100–150). Counting every hobby clone deployed on Vercel or Netlify and every relevant marketplace listing raises the total to **~400–1,000**. The large majority are free; well under 20 charge money; and only about 6 web generators exceed ~75K monthly visits.
