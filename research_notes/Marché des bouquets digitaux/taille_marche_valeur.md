# Market size & value: e-cards, greeting cards, online flowers, virtual/digital gifting and personalized keepsakes, and what they mean for a paid animated digital bouquet (as of October 2026)

Conventions: currencies are given as reported (USD, EUR, GBP, RMB). Any conversions are approximate and labelled as inferences. "[via search extract]" means the page blocked direct fetching (HTTP 403/404), so the figure comes from search-engine extracts of that page and could not be checked against the full page. Market-research-firm figures are directional: firms almost never publish their methodology, and they often disagree by 2-3x.

---

## 1. What do research firms estimate for the global e-card / digital greeting card market, and how consistent are the estimates?

### Takeaway
Published 2026 estimates for the global e-card market range from about **USD 1.8B to USD 5.3B**, with growth forecasts of **4-9% a year**. That is faster than physical cards (about 1-2% a year), but the estimates contradict each other, even within a single publisher. Many of the reports look low quality. Most consumer e-cards are free, so the *paid* consumer e-card pool is probably much smaller than the headline figures.

### Cited Findings
**Dedicated e-card reports (all from Business Research Insights, BRI, which publishes several overlapping reports):**
- BRI "E-Card Market" (report 125285, last updated 14 Sep 2026, base year 2025): **USD 4.97B in 2026 → USD 10.19B by 2035, CAGR 7.44%** (2026-2035). Regional split: North America 40%, Europe 30%, Asia-Pacific 20%. Type split in 2035: birthday 35%, holiday 25%, thank-you 15%, invitations 15%, business e-cards 10% — [BRI E-Card Market](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/e-card-market-125285)
- The same BRI page makes unsourced claims: "over 65% of consumers prefer E-Cards over traditional greeting cards", "Top 10 E-Card service providers account for over 60% of global market share", "Moonpig sends over 10 million e-cards annually", and "Blue Mountain distributes more than 8 million digital cards yearly". It gives no methodology — [BRI E-Card Market](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/e-card-market-125285)
- BRI "Ecard Market" (report 121048, updated 13 Apr 2026): **USD 1.77B in 2026 → USD 3.77B by 2035, CAGR 8.5%**. North America is about 38% of usage, and the "top five platforms" are said to hold more than 55% share. Key players named: Hallmark, Postable, American Greetings, LovePop, Paperless Post — [BRI Ecard Market](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/ecard-market-121048)
- BRI "Personalized E-Greeting Card Market": **USD 1.95B in 2026 → USD 4.06B by 2035, CAGR 8.5%** [via search extract] — [BRI Personalized E-Greeting Card](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/personalized-e-greeting-card-market-123023)

**E-cards inside the overall greeting card reports:**
- Grand View Research (GVR, 2026 edition): global greeting cards **USD 19.8B in 2025, USD 20.1B in 2026 → USD 23.0B by 2033, CAGR 1.9%** (2026-2033). Traditional cards held **73.3%** of 2025 revenue, the offline channel **63.3%**, and North America **42.7%** [via search extract] — [GVR Greeting Cards](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/greeting-cards-market-report)
- Search summaries of the GVR study report the e-card segment growing at about **4.7% CAGR (2025-2033)** [via search extract; not verified on the page] — [GVR segmentation](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/greeting-cards-market-report/segmentation)
- An earlier edition (ResearchAndMarkets release, June 2023): global greeting cards would reach **USD 20.66B by 2030 at 0.9% CAGR** (2023-2030). E-cards were the fastest-growing type at **4.2% CAGR**, and online was the fastest-growing channel — [Businesswire / ResearchAndMarkets 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230609005296/en/Global-Greeting-Cards-Market-Report-2023-Sector-is-Expected-to-Reach-$20.66-Billion-by-2030-at-a-CAGR-of-0.9---ResearchAndMarkets.com)
- Another study, resold by GII: **USD 19.84B in 2025 → USD 24.41B by 2036, CAGR 2.0%** [via search extract] — [GII](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/bzc2092575-global-greeting-cards-market-size-study-forecast.html)

**United States, online:**
- IBISWorld puts the US "Online Greeting Card Sales" industry at **USD 2.6B in 2026**. This industry likely includes printed cards ordered online, not only e-cards — [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/industry-statistics/market-size/online-greeting-card-sales-united-states/)
- An aggregator repeats "U.S. online greeting card sales $2.6B in 2025", with a projected 4.89% CAGR to 2034. This is a secondary source of low reliability — [ProHolidayCheers](https://proholidaycheers.com/digital-greeting-card-statistics/)

**Evidence against "consumers prefer e-cards":**
- UK Greeting Card Association (GCA): online's share of card volume fell from **10.9% to 9.8%** in 2023, and **over 80%** of cards are still bought in physical stores — [PG Buzz / GCA](https://pgbuzz.net/?p=84594)
- US consumers still buy about **6.5 billion** physical greeting cards a year (GCA) — [Gifts & Decorative Accessories](https://www.giftsanddec.com/business-news/surprising-facts-about-cards)

**Prices of paid e-cards (the closest paid product to an animated bouquet):**
- Jacquie Lawson (animated e-cards): **US$8/month, US$36/year or US$50/two years** for unlimited sends, with an address book, reminders and scheduling up to a year ahead. Blue Mountain can be bundled for about **US$18/year** more [via search extract; direct fetch blocked] — [Jacquie Lawson membership](https://www.jacquielawson.com/prices-membership)
- Third-party comparison pages list Blue Mountain at about **US$29.99/yr** and Hallmark eCards at about **US$18/yr**. These pages are low reliability, so treat the figures as unverified — [teck.net guide](https://wp03.teck.net/jackie-lawrence-ecards-n5q5.html)

### Inferences
- **The spread is wide.** 2026 figures go from USD 1.77B to USD 4.97B (2.8x), both from the same publisher, BRI. If GVR splits its market into only two types (traditional vs e-cards), its e-card segment works out to about **26.7% × USD 19.8B ≈ USD 5.3B (2025)**. A reasonable consensus band is **USD 2-5B, growing 4-9%/yr**.
- **The growth rates also disagree.** GVR and ResearchAndMarkets give 4.2-4.7% for e-cards; BRI gives 7.4-8.5%. The BRI pages show signs of low quality: survey percentages with no source, the implausible "65% prefer e-cards" claim (contradicted by GCA channel data), and the description of Moonpig, a printed-card company, as an e-card sender.
- **The headline e-card numbers probably bundle together** consumer e-cards, digital invitations (Paperless Post, Evite), corporate e-greetings and possibly printed cards ordered online. Most consumer e-cards are free or ad-supported, so the **paid consumer animated e-card pool is probably in the hundreds of millions of USD rather than billions**. No direct data confirms this.
- The paid model that has proven itself for premium animated e-cards is a **low-cost annual subscription** (about US$18-36/yr), not high per-send prices.

### Gaps
- Statista, Technavio, Allied Market Research and IMARC figures for e-cards specifically were not accessible (paywalls; Allied's greeting card page returned 403). No IMARC greeting card or e-card report was found.
- None of the paid e-card leaders (American Greetings/Blue Mountain, Jacquie Lawson, Paperless Post, Punchbowl, Hallmark eCards) disclose revenue or subscriber numbers.
- No France-specific data on e-card market size or usage was found.

## 2. How big are the global/US greeting card and online flower delivery markets, and what do US consumers spend on flowers for key occasions?

### Takeaway
The global greeting card market is about **USD 20B and flat (1-2%/yr)**. The US is about **USD 7-8B** (6.5B cards a year). Estimates for online flowers are irreconcilable: **USD 7.7B "global online"** in one report, **USD 12.5-16.3B for US floral gifting/online** in others. US consumers spend about **USD 6.3B on flowers for Valentine's Day and Mother's Day combined** (NRF 2026). The listed online leaders are moving in opposite directions: 1-800-Flowers revenue fell 10.8% while Moonpig grew 6.5%.

### Cited Findings
**Greeting cards (US):**
- GCA: US consumers buy about **6.5B cards a year**, worth **USD 7-8B in annual retail sales**. By season: Christmas about 1.6B cards, Valentine's Day 145M (excluding classroom valentines), Mother's Day 133M, Father's Day 90M, graduation 67M. Women buy 80% of cards. These are long-standing GCA estimates repeated for years, so treat them as dated — [Gifts & Decorative Accessories](https://www.giftsanddec.com/business-news/surprising-facts-about-cards); [USPS News](https://news.usps.com/2019/09/26/facts-on-paper/)

**US occasion spending (NRF / Prosper surveys):**
- Valentine's Day 2026: expected **USD 29.1B** total (record; 2025 was USD 27.5B). Average **USD 199.78 per person** (USD 188.81 in 2025). 55% celebrate. **41% plan to buy flowers, USD 3.1B on flowers.** 41% plan to buy greeting cards. Jewelry USD 7B; evening out USD 6.3B (39%). 38% name online as their main shopping destination. Gifts for pets USD 2.1B, friends USD 2.4B, classmates/teachers USD 2.2B, co-workers USD 1.7B. Sample: 7,791 adults, surveyed 2-8 Jan 2026, ±1.1 pts — [NRF Valentine's 2026](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/valentine-s-day-spending-expected-to-reach-new-records)
- One student newspaper reported USD 29.1B as the *actual* outcome "surpassing a USD 27.5B projection". This mixes up NRF's 2026 projection with the 2025 total. The same article says flower prices rose 6.4% (CPI); that figure is unverified — [Thunderword (Highline College)](https://thunderword.highline.edu/2026/02/19/valentines-day-spending-reaches-record-29-1-billion/)
- Mother's Day 2026: record **USD 38B** expected (USD 34.1B in 2025; previous record USD 35.7B in 2023). Average **USD 284.25 per person**. **75% plan to buy flowers, USD 3.2B on flowers.** 74% plan to buy greeting cards, USD 1.3B on cards. 33% shop online. A record one-third plan to give experiences. **46% look for "something unique or different" and 39% want to "create a special memory"**. Sample: 7,877 adults, surveyed 1-8 Apr 2026 — [NRF Mother's Day 2026](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/mother-s-day-spending-expected-to-hit-record-38-billion)

**Online flower delivery / floral gifting estimates (they conflict):**
- Verified Market Research: global online flower delivery service market **USD 7.70B (2025) → USD 13.23B (2033), CAGR 7.0%** — [VMR](https://www.verifiedmarketresearch.com/product/online-flower-delivery-service-market/)
- The Report Cubes: **US** online flower delivery **USD 16.3B (2025) → USD 28.01B (2034), CAGR 6.20%**. This is double VMR's *global* figure — [The Report Cubes](https://www.thereportcubes.com/public/report-store/online-flower-delivery-market-united-states)
- Arizton: US floral gifting **USD 12.55B (2025) → USD 17.06B (2031), CAGR 5.25%** — [Arizton](https://arizton.com/market-reports/us-floral-gifting-market)
- IMARC: North America floral gifting **USD 15.8B (2024) → USD 25.2B (2033), CAGR 5.06%** — [IMARC](https://imarcgroup.com/north-america-floral-gifting-market)

**Company benchmarks:**
- 1-800-Flowers, fiscal 2026 (year ended about late June 2026): revenue **USD 1.50B, down 10.8%** from USD 1.69B. Q4 revenue USD 293.1M (−12.9%): Consumer Floral & Gifts −13.4%, Gourmet Foods & Gift Baskets −15.4%, BloomNet +1.9%. Q4 gross margin 34.7% — [SEC 8-K, FY2026 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001084869/000108486926000024/flws-fiscal2026xexx99x1xfi.htm). Press coverage says the company is evaluating options to raise capital — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/1-800-flowers-com-fiscal-2026-revenue-falls-10-8-to-1-5-billion-as-company-evaluates-capital-raising-options/)
- Moonpig Group, FY26 (year to 30 Apr 2026): revenue **£373.0M (+6.5%)**. The Moonpig brand grew 8.6%; Greetz (Netherlands) grew 1.5% in constant currency. Orders +2.1%. **Active customers 12.3M** (12.0M a year earlier). **AOV +5.7%**, from "trading-up to higher-priced gifts", larger card formats and tracked delivery. Adjusted EBITDA margin 28.0%; free cash flow £73.5M — [Moonpig FY26 results](https://www.moonpig.group/news-and-media/press-releases/2026-full-year-results-announcement/)
- Bloom & Wild (described as Europe's leading online florist): revenue about **USD 150M in 2024**, down from a peak of USD 191M in 2021 (USD 23M in 2018). Adjusted EBITDA USD 5.6M in 2024. These are Sacra estimates in USD — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/bloom-and-wild/)

### Inferences
- **The online flower estimates cannot all be right.** A US-only figure of USD 16.3B is twice a global figure of USD 7.7B. Definitions differ: online-only vs all floral gifting, whether wire-service orders are included, retail value vs company revenue. The report should give a range and not pick one number.
- **Pandemic-era online floral growth has reversed for some players:** 1-800-Flowers is down 10.8%, and Bloom & Wild is below its 2021 peak. Growth is going to platforms that sell **card + gift bundles and trade customers up** (Moonpig: AOV +5.7%). A digital bouquet may therefore do better as an add-on or upsell on such platforms than as a stand-alone gift.
- The two biggest US flower occasions together are worth about **USD 6.3B** in flower spend (USD 3.1B + 3.2B).
- NRF's motivation data ("unique or different" 46%, "special memory" 39%) supports positioning the product as a keepsake or memory rather than a cheap substitute for flowers.

### Gaps
- No reliable average *per-purchase* flower spend for the US. NRF gives per-person totals across all gifts. Society of American Florists data was not obtained.
- Hallmark (private), American Greetings, FTD and Card Factory figures were not obtained.
- None of the market-research firms used publishes its methodology, so the estimates cannot be reconciled.

## 3. France & Europe: greeting cards, flowers, online share and occasion spend (Saint-Valentin, Fête des mères)

### Takeaway
France has good recent data on flowers (Kantar panel for FranceAgriMer/Val'hor) but almost no recent data on greeting cards; the last public figure is about **€402M in 2010**. French occasion flower spend is small: **about €33M for Saint-Valentin** (1.3M households × €25.20) and **€61.8M for Fête des mères**, roughly **€95M for the two occasions**. Online is a minority channel but growing fast: Saint-Valentin online sales went from **6% to 13%** of purchases in 2025. The UK, Europe's largest card market (about **£1.5B**), still sells about 90% of cards offline by volume.

### Cited Findings
**United Kingdom (largest European card market):**
- GCA UK Market Report (published 8 Nov 2024, data for Jan-Dec 2023): single-card retail value **£1.51B** (+4% vs £1.46B in 2022), about **800M cards**. **Online share of volume fell from 10.9% to 9.8%**, and **over 80%** of cards are bought in physical stores. Christmas was 11.7% of value — [PG Buzz / GCA](https://pgbuzz.net/?p=84594)
- GCA Market Report 2025 (2024 data): single-card value **£1.53B (+1.1%)**, average price **£1.95** (up from £1.89), **782.4M cards** [via search extract; page returned 404] — [GCA](https://www.gca.cards/?p=45882)
- Moonpig Group (UK + Greetz in the Netherlands): FY26 revenue £373.0M, 12.3M active customers; Greetz grew 1.5% in constant currency — [Moonpig FY26](https://www.moonpig.group/news-and-media/press-releases/2026-full-year-results-announcement/)

**France, greeting cards (very dated):**
- Union Professionnelle de la Carte Postale (UPCP): market (postcards + greeting cards + gift wrap) **€402M**, volume −0.5%, value −2.8%. **Q4 brings 44% of greeting card revenue.** Average postcard price €0.40. A 2009 Éditions Yvon/GfK survey of 1,000 households found **70% preferred paper postcards to digital**. These figures date from **2010** (article of 10 Sep 2010) — [Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/carte-postale-deux-societes-en-croissance-sur-un-marche-stable-91163)

**France, flowers & plants (Kantar panel of 7,000 households for FranceAgriMer & Val'hor; primary sources):**
- 2024: **more than 20M households (70%)** bought at least one plant (76% in 2019). **Average annual budget €109 per buying household** (−€9, the lowest since 2019); about €5 per plant. Florists take **27% of spending**. **Distance selling ("vente à distance") is 4% of volume** for all plants — [FranceAgriMer horticulture fact sheet, Jan 2026](https://www.franceagrimer.fr/sites/default/files/2026-02/02_FICHE_FILIERE_HORTICULTURE_2026.pdf)
- Florists' turnover **€1.5B in 2023, 75% from cut flowers** (Xerfi Specific for FranceAgriMer & Val'hor). Garden centres turned over €3.1B in 2024. France is the 5th-largest EU producer (12% of EU value in 2023). Imports were €1.01B in 2024, leaving a trade deficit of €922M — [FranceAgriMer fact sheet](https://www.franceagrimer.fr/sites/default/files/2026-02/02_FICHE_FILIERE_HORTICULTURE_2026.pdf)
- **Saint-Valentin 2025:** **1.3M households** bought flowers or plants (same as 2024). **Average budget €25.20 per buying household** (€25.30 in 2024). Roses were 57% of purchases (58%). **75% of spending went on ready-made bunches or bouquets** (46% in 2024). Florists' share fell to **50%** (57%). **"Online sales double, reaching 13% of purchases, versus 6% last year"** — [Val'hor press release, 29 Jan 2026](https://bo.valhor.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/cp-st-valentin_2026.pdf); [FranceAgriMer St-Valentin 2025 infographic](https://www.franceagrimer.fr/sites/default/files/2025-07/BIL-HOR-St%20Valentin%202025.pdf)
- **Fête des mères 2025:** **2.2M households** bought flowers or plants, worth **€61.8M** in revenue to the sector. **Average budget €16.80**, above 2023 and 2024. Cut flowers were 63% of purchases; roses 23%. Florists had 46% of purchases, garden centres 19%, supermarkets 14% (falling) — [Val'hor press release, 18 May 2026](https://bo.valhor.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/cp-fetes-des-meres_valhor-2026.pdf)
- The sector as a whole (Val'hor): 47,148 companies, €16.1B revenue, 205,497 jobs — [Val'hor press release, Jan 2026](https://bo.valhor.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/cp-st-valentin_2026.pdf)

**French online flower players (revenues are dated):**
- Interflora France: revenue **€130.7M in 2022** [via search extract] — [Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/entreprise/interflora-france)
- Florajet: about **€35M** revenue and about 4,500 affiliated florists (year not stated) — [AC Franchise](https://ac-franchise.com/annuaire/florajet)
- Aquarelle: **€45M** revenue and more than 1M orders in **2021**; about 150 staff; makes its own bouquets in Oise (France) and Madrid — [franco.wiki](https://franco.wiki/fr/Aquarelle.com_Group.html)
- Bloom & Wild bought the French online florist **Bergamotte in 2021**, alongside a £50M raise — [UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/news/uks-bloom-wild-raises-further-50m-funding-and-acquires-french-rival-bergamotte-20210728). Bloom & Wild says the European flower and house-plant market is worth **£22B** (company claim, c. 2019-2021) — [InternetRetailing](https://internetretailing.net/?p=42288)

### Inferences
- **French occasion flowers ≈ €95M for the two main occasions:** Saint-Valentin about €33M (1.3M × €25.20) plus Fête des mères €61.8M. That is roughly 1/60th of the US figure (USD 6.3B). Spend per household is modest (**€17-28**), which limits what a French digital substitute can charge.
- **The Fête des mères numbers are inconsistent:** €61.8M ÷ 2.2M households ≈ **€28 per household**, while the stated "average budget" is €16.80. The €16.80 is probably per purchase, not per household. Flag this when citing.
- Online is growing quickly for occasion flowers (Saint-Valentin 6% → 13%) but is still only 4% of total plant volume. French consumers are traditional (florists are the first channel), but **last-minute and online behaviour is rising** (ready-made bouquets went from 46% to 75% of Saint-Valentin spend). This favours instant digital delivery as a complement.
- The UK card market is about 90% offline by volume despite Moonpig. Digital-only greeting products are still **a niche even in Europe's most digital card market**.
- The French greeting card market is probably **≤ €400M** today, given the 2010 base and a declining category. This is unverified.
- **Seasonality:** 44% of French greeting card revenue falls in Q4, and flower demand peaks on 14 February and the last Sunday of May. A digital bouquet's revenue would be highly seasonal.

### Gaps
- No recent (post-2010) French greeting card market figure, and no French e-card usage statistics.
- No current (2024-2025) revenue for Interflora France, Florajet or Aquarelle.
- No credible Europe-wide market size for greeting cards or online flowers.
- French press figures for *total* Fête des mères gift budgets (about €50-77; Cofidis/CSA and others) appeared in search results but could not be attributed reliably, so they are not used.
- Figures circulating on French florists' digitalisation (about 65% have a website, 47% of those sell online, about 75% belong to a relay network like Interflora) could not be traced to a primary source.

## 4. How big is the virtual gift economy (TikTok / live-stream gifting) and the digital gifting / e-gift card and personalized gift markets?

### Takeaway
Paying for digital gifts is now normal. **TikTok (with Douyin) reached about USD 6B in in-app purchases in 2024**, mostly coins used to tip creators, and about **USD 1.7B in Q2 2025** alone. **Kuaishou's live-streaming revenue was RMB 39.1B in 2025.** These are *social, public* tipping markets, not private keepsakes. The "digital gift card market" figures (USD 0.5T and up) measure gross card value, not revenue, and should not be compared with a bouquet product. Personalized gifts are about **USD 33B, 68% sold online** (GVR).

### Cited Findings
**TikTok / live-stream gifting:**
- Sensor Tower (via TechCrunch, Feb 2025): **TikTok + Douyin became the first non-game app to pass USD 6B in annual IAP revenue (2024)**, up from USD 4.4B in 2023. Q4 2024 set a record at USD 1.9B. Growth came from users buying **coins to tip creators**. Second place was Monopoly GO at USD 2.6B — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/24/tiktok-with-douyin-becomes-first-app-to-reach-6b-in-annual-consumer-spending)
- Sensor Tower: TikTok made about **USD 1.7B IAP in Q2 2025**, its second-best quarter. All non-game apps together made USD 21.1B IAP in Q2 2025 (+24% YoY) — [Sensor Tower Q2 2025 Digital Market Index](https://sensortower.com/blog/q2-2025-digital-market-index)
- Ipsos study commissioned by TikTok (June-July 2025; n=500, including 300 TikTok users): **more than 60,000 US creators** expected to earn more than the median part-time monthly income from LIVE gifts in 2025. **TikTok keeps about 50%.** Gifts cost from **US$0.01 to about US$560**; a "Galaxy" costs about US$15. 68% of TikTok users surveyed had tried gifting — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/12/02/tiktok-live-ipsos-virtual-gifting-data-study/)
- TikTok publicly cited about **US$10M per day** in LIVE revenue in spring-summer 2025 (secondary source) — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/live-gifting-earnings/)
- Kuaishou: live-streaming revenue **RMB 39.1B in 2025** (+5.5% from RMB 37.1B), 27.4% of total revenue [via search extract] — [Kuaishou FY2025 results](https://kuaishou.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kuaishou-technology-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025)
- Unverified aggregator figures: global live-stream virtual gifting "USD 14.8B", "TikTok LIVE USD 3.8B in gift revenue", "+21.5% YoY". No primary source is given — [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/virtual-gifting-statistics-2026)

**Digital gift cards / e-gifting:**
- IMARC (published 3 Jul 2026): global digital gift card market **USD 513.79B (2025) → USD 1,727.34B (2034), CAGR 12.79%**. Closed-loop cards are 57.3%; B2C is 61.1% (the rest is B2B incentives and rewards); retail/personal gifting is 68.4% of end use; North America 42.9% — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/digital-gift-card-market)
- Other gift-card estimates (all formats) disagree sharply: "over USD 3.81 trillion by 2034" — [GlobeNewswire, Aug 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/8/29/3141633/0/en/Gift-Cards-Market-Size-to-Exceed-USD-3-81-Trillion-by-2034-Driven-by-Digital-Payments-and-E-Commerce-Growth.html); "US$2,220.6B by 2033" — [Persistence Market Research, Jun 2026](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20260604EN76244)

**Personalized gifts:**
- Grand View Research: global personalized gifts **USD 32.7B (2025) → USD 34.9B (2026) → USD 62.5B (2035), CAGR 6.7%**. North America 48%; **the online channel about 68%**; clothing about 32% [via search extract] — [GVR Personalized Gifts](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/personalized-gifts-market-report)
- Another release projects **5.4% CAGR through 2035** — [WBOC press release](https://www.wboc.com/online_features/press_releases/personalized-gifts-market-surges-as-customization-demand-drives-5-4-cagr-through-2035/article_8a99d599-34a1-5cb5-a3d6-52531549e0b8.html)
- Experience gifting: a record one-third of US Mother's Day shoppers planned to give experiences in 2026 — [NRF](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/mother-s-day-spending-expected-to-hit-record-38-billion)

### Inferences
- Virtual gifting is large: TikTok IAP is above USD 6B/yr, and Kuaishou's live streaming is about **USD 5.4B** at roughly 7.2 RMB/USD (approximate conversion). This shows **mass willingness to pay for intangible, ephemeral gifts**. The motive differs from a bouquet, though: these are public, performative gifts to creators. Sensor Tower's IAP totals also include non-gift purchases and Douyin's China revenue, so they **overstate gifting alone**.
- Gift card "market size" is **value loaded onto cards, not revenue**, and includes B2B incentives. It shows that digital gifting is accepted but is **not a usable TAM** for a bouquet product.
- With about 68% of personalized gifts sold online, **personalization is already an online behaviour**, which is relevant to a customizable animated bouquet.

### Gaps
- TikTok does not disclose gifting revenue. A primary Sensor Tower full-year 2025 figure for TikTok IAP was not retrieved (only Q2 2025).
- There is no credible, methodology-backed total for global live-stream gifting.
- No data was found on *virtual flower* gifts specifically (for example, volumes of TikTok's flower-type gifts or digital flower stickers).

There is a clear price ladder. **Micro digital gifts cost US$0.01-15** (TikTok). **Subscription animated e-cards cost about US$36/yr** (Jacquie Lawson). **Character or AI videos cost about US$25-30** (Cameo Kids). **Personalized books cost about US$30-65** (Wonderbly). **Custom digital portraits cost about €50-175+.** **Human-made custom songs cost about US$199** (Songfinch). **Celebrity videos range from under US$25 to thousands.** Prices rise with human craft and personalization, and buying as a gift is the dominant use (80% of Cameo users).

## 6. What does this imply for a realistic serviceable addressable market (SAM) for a premium animated digital bouquet sold online (globally and in France)?

### Takeaway
The adjacent pools are large: greeting cards about USD 20B, online flowers USD 7.7-16B depending on definition, personalized gifts about USD 33B, and TikTok gifting above USD 6B. But a **realistic SAM for a paid premium animated digital bouquet is small: roughly USD 20-150M a year globally and about €0.5-5M a year in France.** It is driven by occasion gifting (Valentine's Day and Mother's Day), online-first and long-distance givers, and paid e-card users. Every figure in this section is an illustrative inference built from the sources cited.

### Cited Findings (anchors used)
- Global e-card market **USD 1.77-4.97B (2026)** — [BRI Ecard](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/ecard-market-121048); [BRI E-Card](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/e-card-market-125285)
- US flower spend on two occasions: **USD 3.1B (Valentine's Day) + USD 3.2B (Mother's Day)** — [NRF Valentine's](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/valentine-s-day-spending-expected-to-reach-new-records); [NRF Mother's Day](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/mother-s-day-spending-expected-to-hit-record-38-billion)
- France: **1.3M households × €25.20** (Saint-Valentin 2025), **13% bought online** — [Val'hor](https://bo.valhor.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/cp-st-valentin_2026.pdf); **2.2M households, €61.8M** (Fête des mères 2025) — [Val'hor](https://bo.valhor.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/cp-fetes-des-meres_valhor-2026.pdf)
- UK online share of card volume about **9.8%** — [PG Buzz/GCA](https://pgbuzz.net/?p=84594)
- Paid animated e-card price about **US$36/yr** — [Jacquie Lawson](https://www.jacquielawson.com/prices-membership)
- Online gifting platform scale: Moonpig **12.3M active customers**, £373.0M revenue — [Moonpig](https://www.moonpig.group/news-and-media/press-releases/2026-full-year-results-announcement/)

### Inferences
**Illustrative SAM scenarios (all assumptions are explicit; none are sourced forecasts):**
- **A. Share of occasion flower spend (as a substitute or add-on):**
  - US: USD 6.3B × 0.5-2% → **USD 32-126M**.
  - France: about €95M (€32.8M Saint-Valentin + €61.8M Fête des mères) × 0.5-2% → **€0.5-1.9M**.
- **B. Niche share of the e-card market:** USD 1.77-4.97B × 1-3% → **USD 18-149M** globally.
- **C. Bottom-up for France:** 3.5M occasion purchases (1.3M + 2.2M, with some household overlap) × 2-5% adoption × €5-10 → **€0.35-1.75M** for the two occasions. Adding birthdays, anniversaries, condolences and Q4 greetings, with an unknown multiplier of perhaps 2-3x, gives **about €1-5M**.
- **The approaches converge on an order of magnitude:** a global SAM in the **tens of millions of USD** (about USD 20-150M) and a **French SAM in the low single-digit millions of euros**. A new entrant's realistic obtainable share in its first years would be a small fraction of that, roughly 1-5% of SAM.

**Why the SAM is much smaller than the adjacent markets:**
- (i) Most e-cards are free, so paid conversion is low.
- (ii) A digital bouquet sells at a fraction of a physical bouquet's price (€3-15 vs €17-28 in France, and much more in the US), so even a meaningful *unit* share is a small *value* share.
- (iii) Even in the UK, the most digital card market, physical channels hold about 90% of volume. French consumers still favour florists (46-50% of occasion purchases).
- (iv) Revenue would be very seasonal: 14 February, Mother's Day (late May in France, May in the US) and Q4.

**What would raise or lower the estimate:**
- Upside: distribution through existing gifting platforms (as an add-on alongside Moonpig-style card + gift baskets, or on florists' and relay networks' sites, offered as an instant message before a physical delivery); corporate e-greetings (10% of BRI's e-card mix); and the fast growth of online occasion flowers in France (Saint-Valentin online share 6% → 13%).
- Downside: free alternatives (stickers, GIFs, free e-cards), and the view that digital flowers are "cheap tokens". TikTok's gift catalogue starts at US$0.01, which anchors low prices for virtual items.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence exists on paid adoption or conversion for animated digital bouquets, so every SAM figure above is a scenario, not a measurement.
- The e-card estimates behind scenario B are low quality and inconsistent. Scenario A assumes the US and France are representative and leaves out other markets (UK, DACH, Benelux) for lack of comparable occasion-spend data.
- No data was found on how many French or European consumers send paid e-cards, which is needed to size a subscription-based SAM.
