2. What a bouquet is

A bouquet is a one-of-a-kind digital artwork: flowers drawn in vector graphics, in a vase, recorded as a non-fungible token (NFT) on the Base network. Each bouquet’s page shows its name, its vase, its number of stems and its price before you buy.

A bouquet lives three months from its minting: it blooms and sways, and a countdown shows the time left. Then it fades and stays faded and still, for good. This is part of the artwork, not a defect.

A bouquet is a collectible. It gives no right to any income or share.

3. The bouquet is yours

Holding its token, you own the bouquet: you may show it wherever you like, keep it, give it or resell it, with no other formality. As with a painting, buying it does not transfer the copyright in the drawing: making and selling copies or merchandise of it is not included.

4. Price

The price is set in ETH, with no VAT (VAT not applicable, art. 293 B of the French General Tax Code), and shown on the bouquet before you buy. The amount shown beside it in euros or US dollars is the same price at the current rate, read from CoinGecko; you pay the ETH price. The Base network’s fees (“gas”) are added by your wallet and paid to the network, not to us.

5. Order and payment

You pay from your own wallet, on this site, through Seaport, the protocol of the OpenSea marketplace where our bouquets are listed. Before paying, you tick a box to ask for immediate delivery (see article 7), then confirm; your wallet then shows the exact amount, and confirming in your wallet places the order with an obligation to pay. A transaction confirmed on the network cannot be cancelled.

You are responsible for your wallet, its keys and the address you buy with. We never hold your keys or your funds.

6. Delivery and receipt

The bouquet is transferred to your wallet in the same transaction, as soon as the network confirms it. The site then gives you a receipt to download, which confirms the purchase, its price, the transaction and your request for immediate delivery. The transaction’s record on the Base network is also proof of the purchase.

What it does with data: it keeps on your computer a random code for this computer, the address of the wallet you link and your settings. The app is provided as is. Uninstalling it (Windows Settings, Apps) removes it and its settings.
