2. The bouquet

A bouquet is a digital work: flowers drawn in vector graphics, in a vase, animated on screen. Each bouquet belongs to a month’s collection and is sold only in a limited number of copies, shown under its name with the number of copies left. The bouquet you buy is exactly the one you see, with no variation. Its page shows, before purchase, its name, its vase, its collection and its price.

A bouquet lives thirty days from its purchase: it blooms and sways, and a countdown shows the time left. It then fades and stays faded and still for good. This change is part of the work and is not a defect.

A bouquet gives no right to any income or share, and cannot be resold on this site.
