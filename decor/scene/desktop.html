Your bouquet on your desktop

Set the bouquet you collected on your computer’s desktop, with Belai Bureau, a free widget for Windows.

In five steps

  1. Download and install Belai Bureau.
  2. Link your wallet from the Belai icon next to the clock.
  3. Choose your bouquet and put it on your screen: one signature, free, no transaction.
  4. Place it, size it, set it in front of or behind your windows, then pin it with ✓.
  5. To move it again, hold the click on the bouquet for six seconds.
Download Belai BureauWindows 10 and 11 · 3 MB · free

Only the wallet that holds a bouquet can show it. On first launch, Windows may warn that it does not know the app yet: “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The app itself is in French for now.
