About

Bouquets drawn flower by flower, that bloom before your eyes.

Flowers drawn from life

Every flower in this garden is drawn in code, line by line, from real photos: the shape of the petals, their colours measured on each variety, the buds, the open flower and the faded one. More than five hundred species are already in the garden.

Bouquets composed stem by stem

Each bouquet is composed stem by stem, each in a real vase, like the bronze carp. The place of every stem, flower and leaf is found in no other bouquet.

A living bouquet

A bouquet lives thirty days from its purchase: a countdown in hours and minutes shows the time it has left. Then it stays faded and still, for good.

The price of real stems

A bouquet costs what its stems would cost at a florist in France. The vase comes free. You pay by card.

A collection every month

Every month has its collection. Each bouquet in it comes in a limited number of copies, shown under its name, and the bouquet you buy is exactly the one you see.

Giving a bouquet

When you buy, you can give the bouquet: a short message carried by the insects, a piece of music, and a code to pass on. The person you give it to receives it in “My gifts” with your first name and that code.

On your desktop

With Belai Bureau, a free widget for Windows, the bouquet you collected stands on your computer’s desktop, at the size you choose, in front of or behind your windows.

Growing the garden

Every bouquet collected helps the garden grow: new species, new vases, new bouquets.

Questions

What is a digital bouquet?
A bouquet of flowers drawn in vector graphics from real photos, stem by stem, in a real vase. It blooms on screen, sways in the breeze and fades after thirty days.
Can I put a flower bouquet on my screen?
Yes. Download Belai Bureau (free), sign in once with your phone number and the code sent by text message: the bouquets of your account settle on your screen, where you place them, size them and pin them.
How is a bouquet priced?
At the sum of what its stems would cost at a florist in France. The vase comes free. The price is shown in euros (in US dollars outside the euro area) and paid by card.
What happens after thirty days?
The bouquet fades and stays faded, still, for good. It remains yours.
How do I give a bouquet?
When you buy, choose to give it: write a short message, pick a piece of music and the insects that will carry it. You get a link and a code; the person enters them in “My gifts” with your first name, and the bouquet moves to their account.
How many copies of each bouquet?
Each bouquet of the month’s collection comes in a limited number of copies, shown under its name. Once they are gone, there are no more.
