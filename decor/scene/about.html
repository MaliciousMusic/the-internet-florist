About

Bouquets drawn flower by flower, that bloom before your eyes.

Flowers drawn from life

Every flower in this garden is drawn in code, line by line, from real photos: the shape of the petals, their colours measured on each variety, the buds, the open flower and the faded one. More than five hundred species are already in the garden.

One-of-a-kind bouquets every day

Every day, new bouquets are composed, each in a real vase, like the bronze carp. Every bouquet is one of a kind: the place of each stem, each flower and each leaf is found in no other.

A living bouquet

A bouquet lives three months from its minting: a countdown, in hours and minutes, shows how long it has left. Then it stays faded and still, for good.

The price of real stems

A bouquet’s price is the sum of what its stems cost at a florist in France. The vase comes free. On resale, we take nothing: 0% royalties.

Collecting a bouquet

The bouquets are NFTs on the Base network, listed on our OpenSea page. You will pay for them right here, from your wallet, without leaving the site.

On your desktop

With Belai Bureau, a free widget for Windows, the bouquet you collected stands on your computer’s desktop, at the size you choose, in front of or behind your windows.

Growing the garden

Every bouquet collected helps the garden grow: new species, new vases, new bouquets.

Questions

What is a digital bouquet?
A bouquet of flowers drawn in vector graphics from real photos, stem by stem, in a real vase. It blooms on screen, sways in the breeze, and fades after three months. Each one is unique and is collected as an NFT on Base.
Can I put a flower bouquet on my computer’s desktop?
Yes. Download Belai Bureau (free, Windows 10 and 11), link your wallet, and the bouquet you own stands on your desktop as a widget: you place it, size it, pin it, and set it in front of or behind your windows.
How is a bouquet priced?
At the sum of what its stems would cost at a florist in France, converted to ETH, with no VAT (VAT not applicable, art. 293 B of the French General Tax Code). The vase comes free. The amount shown in euros or US dollars is an indication at the current rate.
What happens after three months?
The bouquet fades and stays faded, still, for good. It remains yours.
Do you take royalties on resale?
No: 0%. Only the marketplace’s and the network’s own fees apply.
Who can show a bouquet?
Belai Bureau only shows a bouquet held by the wallet you link, after a free signature (no transaction).
